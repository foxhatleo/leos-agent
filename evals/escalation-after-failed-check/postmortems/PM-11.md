# PM-11: delayed reset emails

Date: 2026-07-22
Facilitator: platform reliability

## What happened

A change to shared infrastructure behaved differently in production than it did in the test environment.
The team noticed within the first hour and mitigated before the end of the working day.

## Impact

Customers in the EU received password-reset emails roughly an hour late.

## Contributing factors

- The change was reviewed, but the reviewer did not have access to production metrics.
- The runbook described the old topology.

## Actions

- Update the runbook.
- Add the missing dashboard to the review checklist.
