# INC-1011: reviews degradation

Severity: SEV-2
Service: reviews
Detected: 2026-02-03 09:04 UTC
Author: on-call engineer for reviews

## Summary

Shortly after a routine change reached production, a share of requests returned stale or empty results for the reviews service.
Alerting paged the on-call engineer, who opened this incident and pulled in the owning team.

## Timeline

- 09:00 Change reached production.
- 09:04 First alert fired: a share of requests returned stale or empty results.
- 09:09 On-call acknowledged the page and opened an incident channel.
- 09:15 Dashboards confirmed the impact was limited to reviews.
- 09:22 The owning team joined and began comparing the change against the previous state.
- 09:31 Mitigation started.
- 09:47 Metrics back within normal range; incident moved to monitoring.

## Root cause

A configuration cleanup removed the environment variable that pointed the service at its read replica.
Nothing in pre-production exercised this path at production scale, so the problem only appeared under real traffic.

## Resolution

We did not roll back: the team patched the value in place and restarted the affected pods.

## Follow-ups

- Add an alert on the leading indicator so the next occurrence pages earlier.
- Extend the pre-production checklist for reviews changes.
- Review the runbook entry for this failure mode.
