# INC-1016: analytics degradation

Severity: SEV-1
Service: analytics
Detected: 2026-07-03 14:04 UTC
Author: on-call engineer for analytics

## Summary

Shortly after a routine change reached production, queue depth grew faster than workers could drain it for the analytics service.
Alerting paged the on-call engineer, who opened this incident and pulled in the owning team.

## Timeline

- 14:00 Change reached production.
- 14:04 First alert fired: queue depth grew faster than workers could drain it.
- 14:09 On-call acknowledged the page and opened an incident channel.
- 14:15 Dashboards confirmed the impact was limited to analytics.
- 14:22 The owning team joined and began comparing the change against the previous state.
- 14:31 Mitigation started.
- 14:47 Metrics back within normal range; incident moved to monitoring.

## Root cause

A new query in the orders module was missing its index-friendly predicate and scanned the whole table.
Nothing in pre-production exercised this path at production scale, so the problem only appeared under real traffic.

## Resolution

We rolled the deploy back to the previous release, and error rates returned to baseline within minutes.

## Follow-ups

- Add an alert on the leading indicator so the next occurrence pages earlier.
- Extend the pre-production checklist for analytics changes.
- Review the runbook entry for this failure mode.
