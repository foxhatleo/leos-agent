# INC-1012: catalog degradation

Severity: SEV-3
Service: catalog
Detected: 2026-03-08 10:04 UTC
Author: on-call engineer for catalog

## Summary

Shortly after a routine change reached production, login attempts started timing out for the catalog service.
Alerting paged the on-call engineer, who opened this incident and pulled in the owning team.

## Timeline

- 10:00 Change reached production.
- 10:04 First alert fired: login attempts started timing out.
- 10:09 On-call acknowledged the page and opened an incident channel.
- 10:15 Dashboards confirmed the impact was limited to catalog.
- 10:22 The owning team joined and began comparing the change against the previous state.
- 10:31 Mitigation started.
- 10:47 Metrics back within normal range; incident moved to monitoring.

## Root cause

A serializer change in the release wrote timestamps without a timezone offset.
Nothing in pre-production exercised this path at production scale, so the problem only appeared under real traffic.

## Resolution

The change was rolled back across all regions and the service recovered as soon as the rollback finished.

## Follow-ups

- Add an alert on the leading indicator so the next occurrence pages earlier.
- Extend the pre-production checklist for catalog changes.
- Review the runbook entry for this failure mode.
