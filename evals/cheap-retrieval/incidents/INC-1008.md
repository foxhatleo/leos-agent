# INC-1008: profile degradation

Severity: SEV-2
Service: profile
Detected: 2026-08-13 15:04 UTC
Author: on-call engineer for profile

## Summary

Shortly after a routine change reached production, the error rate rose to twelve percent of requests for the profile service.
Alerting paged the on-call engineer, who opened this incident and pulled in the owning team.

## Timeline

- 15:00 Change reached production.
- 15:04 First alert fired: the error rate rose to twelve percent of requests.
- 15:09 On-call acknowledged the page and opened an incident channel.
- 15:15 Dashboards confirmed the impact was limited to profile.
- 15:22 The owning team joined and began comparing the change against the previous state.
- 15:31 Mitigation started.
- 15:47 Metrics back within normal range; incident moved to monitoring.

## Root cause

A feature-flag change enabled the new cache path in every region at once instead of the canary region only.
Nothing in pre-production exercised this path at production scale, so the problem only appeared under real traffic.

## Resolution

On-call reverted to the last known good build through the deploy pipeline, which restored normal behaviour.

## Follow-ups

- Add an alert on the leading indicator so the next occurrence pages earlier.
- Extend the pre-production checklist for profile changes.
- Review the runbook entry for this failure mode.
