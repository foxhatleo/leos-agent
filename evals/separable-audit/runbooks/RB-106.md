# RB-106: gateway service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 3, so an outage waits for business hours. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Procedure

1. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
2. Rollbacks go through the deploy pipeline; never edit production by hand.
3. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
