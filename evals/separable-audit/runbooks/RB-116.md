# RB-116: sessions service runbook

## Scope

The service is classed as tier 2; outages page the owning team only. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.

## Procedure

1. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.
2. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
3. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
