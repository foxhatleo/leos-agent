# RB-104: checkout service runbook

## Scope

An outage here pages nobody; it is a tier 3 internal tool. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Procedure

1. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
2. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
3. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
