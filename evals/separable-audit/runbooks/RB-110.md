# RB-110: notifications service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 1: an outage pages the incident commander immediately. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Procedure

1. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
2. Rollbacks go through the deploy pipeline; never edit production by hand.
3. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
