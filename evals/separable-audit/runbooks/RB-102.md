# RB-102: billing service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 3, so an outage waits for business hours. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.

## Procedure

1. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
2. Rollbacks go through the deploy pipeline; never edit production by hand.
3. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Maintenance
