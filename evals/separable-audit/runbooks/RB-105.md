# RB-105: email service runbook

## Scope

The service is classed as tier 1, so any outage is handled around the clock. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.

## Procedure

1. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
2. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.
3. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
