# RB-113: profile service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 3, so an outage waits for business hours. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.

## Procedure

1. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
2. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
3. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
