# RB-118: wishlist service runbook

## Scope

An outage here pages nobody; it is a tier 3 internal tool. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.

## Procedure

1. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
2. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.
3. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
