# RB-114: reports service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 1: an outage pages the incident commander immediately. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.

## Procedure

1. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
2. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
3. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Maintenance

Its last review was in January 2026.
