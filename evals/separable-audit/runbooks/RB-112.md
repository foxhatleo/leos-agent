# RB-112: payments service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 1: an outage pages the incident commander immediately. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.

## Procedure

1. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
2. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
3. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
