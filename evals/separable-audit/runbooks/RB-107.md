# RB-107: inventory service runbook

## Scope

This service is tier 1: an outage pages the incident commander immediately. The service has two replicas per region behind the load balancer.

## Procedure

1. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
2. If the database is the bottleneck, follow the connection-pool section below.
3. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
