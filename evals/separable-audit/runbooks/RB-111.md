# RB-111: orders service runbook

## Scope

The service is classed as tier 2; outages page the owning team only. Rollbacks go through the deploy pipeline; never edit production by hand.

## Procedure

1. Escalate to the owning team's channel when the first two steps do not help.
2. The first step is to check the service dashboard for error rate and latency.
3. Record every action in the incident channel with a timestamp.
