# T-2109: seat billing

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-08-05
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up.

## Outcome

The first-response time was within the published target. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one.
