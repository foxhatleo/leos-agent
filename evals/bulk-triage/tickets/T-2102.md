# T-2102: charge after cancellation

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-08-11
Channel: chat

## Customer report

The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support.

## Investigation

Engineering confirmed a proration defect in the billing system that overcharged the account. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel.

## Outcome

The first-response time was within the published target. We refunded the overcharged amount and confirmed it with the customer. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review.
