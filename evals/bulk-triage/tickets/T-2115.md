# T-2115: seat billing

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-09-10
Channel: chat

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder.

## Investigation

The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. No refund was issued; we sent a corrected invoice instead.
