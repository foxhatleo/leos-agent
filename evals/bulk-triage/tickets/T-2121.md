# T-2121: invoice question

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-02
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint. The first-response time was within the published target. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed.
