# T-2134: unexpected charge

Product area: subscriptions
Opened: 2026-08-01
Channel: chat

## Customer report

Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement.

## Investigation

The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer.

## Outcome

The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The first-response time was within the published target.
