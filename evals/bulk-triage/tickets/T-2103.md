# T-2103: charge after cancellation

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-08-09
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement.

## Investigation

The investigation found the billing service charged the renewal twice because of a retry bug. The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel.

## Outcome

The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed.
