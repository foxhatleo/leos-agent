# T-2107: tax on invoice

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-09
Channel: email

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support.

## Investigation

A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up. Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period.

## Outcome

A refund was offered, but the customer chose to keep the amount as account credit instead. The first-response time was within the published target. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure.
