# T-2124: duplicate payment

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-09-07
Channel: email

## Customer report

They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. We refunded the overcharged amount and confirmed it with the customer.
