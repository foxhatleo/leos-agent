# T-2106: tax on invoice

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-08-25
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder. They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review.
