# T-2135: tax on invoice

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-09-15
Channel: email

## Customer report

They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer.

## Outcome

Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The first-response time was within the published target.
