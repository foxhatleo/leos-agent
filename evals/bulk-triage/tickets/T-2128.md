# T-2128: unexpected charge

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-08-07
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder.

## Investigation

The charge was correct: the customer had added three seats earlier in the month. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period.

## Outcome

Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer.
