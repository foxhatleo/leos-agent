# T-2132: charge after cancellation

Product area: subscriptions
Opened: 2026-09-04
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support.

## Investigation

The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. Engineering confirmed a proration defect in the billing system that overcharged the account. The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. Finance approved a refund of the difference, and it was paid out within the week. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed.
