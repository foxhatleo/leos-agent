# T-2122: invoice question

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-06
Channel: email

## Customer report

They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. A currency-conversion bug in the billing system rounded the charge up by a full unit.

## Outcome

A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. Support issued a full refund to the original card the same day.
