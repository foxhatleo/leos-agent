# T-2113: charge after cancellation

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-09-23
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The invoice generator applied last year's tax rate, a defect in the billing system. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The first-response time was within the published target. Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint.
