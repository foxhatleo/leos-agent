# T-2131: duplicate payment

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-07-09
Channel: chat

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The investigation found the billing service charged the renewal twice because of a retry bug.

## Outcome

Finance approved a refund of the difference, and it was paid out within the week. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. The first-response time was within the published target.
