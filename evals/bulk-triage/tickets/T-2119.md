# T-2119: invoice question

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-14
Channel: email

## Customer report

The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. The customer's bank declined the first attempt, and the second, valid charge went through as designed.

## Outcome

The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. A refund was offered, but the customer chose to keep the amount as account credit instead.
