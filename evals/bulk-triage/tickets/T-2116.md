# T-2116: unexpected charge

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-08-17
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement.

## Investigation

A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The charge was correct: the customer had added three seats earlier in the month.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The first-response time was within the published target. We refunded the overcharged amount and confirmed it with the customer.
