# T-2120: invoice question

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-09-17
Channel: chat

## Customer report

Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support.

## Investigation

We first suspected a billing defect, but the logs showed the customer had upgraded the plan themselves. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline.

## Outcome

The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review.
