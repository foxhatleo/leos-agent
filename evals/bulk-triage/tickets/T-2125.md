# T-2125: tax on invoice

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-08-07
Channel: email

## Customer report

The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The charge was correct: the customer had added three seats earlier in the month.

## Outcome

A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The first-response time was within the published target. We refunded the overcharged amount and confirmed it with the customer.
