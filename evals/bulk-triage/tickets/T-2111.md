# T-2111: unexpected charge

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-07-20
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number.

## Investigation

We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one. The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles.

## Outcome

The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint.
