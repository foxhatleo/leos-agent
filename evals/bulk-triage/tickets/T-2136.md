# T-2136: amount looks wrong

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-07-10
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The billing system kept charging a cancelled plan because of a state-sync defect. A second engineer reviewed the findings before anything was communicated to the customer.

## Outcome

The first-response time was within the published target. Support issued a full refund to the original card the same day. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer.
