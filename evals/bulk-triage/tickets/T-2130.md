# T-2130: tax on invoice

Product area: subscriptions
Opened: 2026-09-03
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation.

## Investigation

We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The invoice generator applied last year's tax rate, a defect in the billing system.

## Outcome

A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. No refund was issued; we sent a corrected invoice instead.
