# T-2108: tax on invoice

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-07-24
Channel: chat

## Customer report

The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles.

## Investigation

Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The customer's bank declined the first attempt, and the second, valid charge went through as designed.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. Support issued a full refund to the original card the same day.
