# T-2127: unexpected charge

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-09-21
Channel: email

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number.

## Investigation

A currency-conversion bug in the billing system rounded the charge up by a full unit. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. No refund was issued; we sent a corrected invoice instead. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer.
