# T-2101: amount looks wrong

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-09-12
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number.

## Investigation

We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. A currency-conversion bug in the billing system rounded the charge up by a full unit. Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period.

## Outcome

The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure.
