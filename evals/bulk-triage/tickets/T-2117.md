# T-2117: unexpected charge

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-07-25
Channel: email

## Customer report

The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement. Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation. The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder.

## Investigation

The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. A currency-conversion bug in the billing system rounded the charge up by a full unit. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

The customer asked for a refund; we explained the charge and closed the ticket without one. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure.
