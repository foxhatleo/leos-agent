# T-2112: charge after cancellation

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-07-02
Channel: chat

## Customer report

Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The charge was correct: the customer had added three seats earlier in the month. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. A refund was offered, but the customer chose to keep the amount as account credit instead.
