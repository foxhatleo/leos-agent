# T-2118: amount looks wrong

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-08-05
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The customer wrote in through the help center after noticing the charge on their card statement.

## Investigation

Engineering confirmed a proration defect in the billing system that overcharged the account. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline.

## Outcome

The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. The first-response time was within the published target. Finance approved a refund of the difference, and it was paid out within the week.
