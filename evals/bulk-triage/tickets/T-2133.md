# T-2133: charge after cancellation

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-28
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation.

## Investigation

The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. We first suspected a billing defect, but the logs showed the customer had upgraded the plan themselves.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. Support issued a full refund to the original card the same day.
