# T-2129: invoice question

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-07-22
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. We first suspected a billing defect, but the logs showed the customer had upgraded the plan themselves. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. Support discussed refunding the charge but closed the ticket once the customer withdrew the complaint.
