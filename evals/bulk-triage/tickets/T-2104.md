# T-2104: charge after cancellation

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-07-07
Channel: phone

## Customer report

The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket. The message was polite but asked for an answer before the end of the week. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected.

## Investigation

Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period. The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline.

## Outcome

Finance approved a refund of the difference, and it was paid out within the week. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure. The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review.
