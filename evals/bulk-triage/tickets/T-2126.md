# T-2126: invoice question

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-08-09
Channel: phone

## Customer report

They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The investigation found the billing service charged the renewal twice because of a retry bug.

## Outcome

The first-response time was within the published target. The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. A refund was offered, but the customer chose to keep the amount as account credit instead.
