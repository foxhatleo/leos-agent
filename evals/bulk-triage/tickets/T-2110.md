# T-2110: seat billing

Product area: payments
Opened: 2026-09-21
Channel: chat

## Customer report

The ticket arrived through the chat widget and was converted to email by the first responder. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. Their finance team flagged the amount during a monthly reconciliation.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. The payments provider's dashboard showed the transaction timeline. The annual renewal ran on the date the customer agreed to when they signed up.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The first-response time was within the published target. Finance approved a refund of the difference, and it was paid out within the week.
