# T-2114: tax on invoice

Product area: invoicing
Opened: 2026-08-21
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected. They attached a screenshot of the invoice and asked us to explain the line items. The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket.

## Investigation

The billing team was looped in through the usual escalation channel. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

The macro used in the reply was updated afterwards to be clearer. No refund was issued; we sent a corrected invoice instead. A satisfaction survey was sent after closure.
