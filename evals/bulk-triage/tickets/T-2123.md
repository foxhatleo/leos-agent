# T-2123: amount looks wrong

Product area: seats
Opened: 2026-09-20
Channel: chat

## Customer report

They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles. The customer has been on the platform for several years and rarely contacts support. They asked whether other customers on the same plan had been affected.

## Investigation

Engineering confirmed a proration defect in the billing system that overcharged the account. The support engineer pulled the account's billing history for the last three cycles. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge.

## Outcome

The first-response time was within the published target. The customer replied to thank the team, and the ticket was closed. We refunded the overcharged amount and confirmed it with the customer.
