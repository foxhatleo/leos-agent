# T-2105: renewal dispute

Product area: tax
Opened: 2026-09-28
Channel: email

## Customer report

The account owner was travelling, so a colleague with billing access opened the ticket. They included the last four digits of the card and the invoice number. They mentioned that the same thing had not happened in previous billing cycles.

## Investigation

Nothing unusual appeared in the account's audit log for that period. We compared the invoice against the plan and seat counts recorded at the time of the charge. The customer had two accounts and was looking at the invoice for the other one.

## Outcome

The ticket was tagged for the monthly billing review. The first-response time was within the published target. No refund was issued; we sent a corrected invoice instead.
